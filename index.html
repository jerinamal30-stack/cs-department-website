About the Department

The Department of Computer Science was established in 2005 and has grown into one of the most active departments of the college. We combine strong theoretical foundations with hands-on, project-based learning so that every graduate is ready for industry or higher studies.

Our mission is to nurture skilled, ethical and creative computing professionals. H2O may be a chemistry formula, but at our department we write x2 + y2 = z2 in algorithms every day. Students use open-source tools and write code such as print("Hello, World!") from their very first week.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in computing education, research and community service.

Our Mission

To deliver quality teaching, encourage innovation, and build strong industry links.

Academic Programmes

The department offers the following undergraduate and postgraduate programmes:

  • B.Sc. Computer Science
  • B.C.A. (Bachelor of Computer Applications)
  • M.Sc. Computer Science
  • M.C.A. (Master of Computer Applications)
  • Diploma in Web Development

Subjects Offered

  1. Programming in C and Python
  2. Data Structures and Algorithms
  3. Database Management Systems
  4. Web Technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
  5. Operating Systems
  6. Computer Networks
  7. Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
